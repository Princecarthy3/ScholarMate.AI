Install ScholarMate AI App

🍏 iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. Open https://scholarmate-ai.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add at top right corner.
🤖 Android (Google Chrome)
  1. Open https://scholarmate-ai.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the 3 dots menu (⋮) at top right corner.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm installation to place the ScholarMate icon on your launcher!
